    Biography

    Aldric is the 42nd monarch of Northwhaldia. Known as the “Sage of the Terminal,” he is famous for his unconventional method of ruling his domain entirely via plain-text files and shell scripts.

    He shares his throne with Queen Elysia, who oversees the kingdom’s cryptographic defenses. Unlike her husband, Elysia prefers a more “Debian-stable” approach to governance, leading to lively midnight debates in the royal court.

    Early Life

    Aldric’s birth in the royal house of Northstar is often treated as the beginning of the modern Northwald succession.

    Conflict with the South

    Currently, Aldric’s forces are engaged in a long-standing border dispute with South Isles. This war began not over land or gold, but over a disagreement regarding the default init system for the regional network.

    Equipment and Tools

    Aldric is rarely seen without his enchanted mechanical keyboard, forged in the fires of Mount Git. It is said that when he types, the very fabric of the kingdom’s configuration files updates in real-time.
