    Biography

    Queen Elysia is the co-ruler of Northwaldia. Known throughout the realms as “The Keeper of the Keys,” she manages the kingdom’s vast cryptographic archives and ensure the stability of the realm’s core services.

    While her husband, King Aldric, focuses on rapid development and experimental “rolling” policies, Elysia is the voice of caution. She famously refused to update the kingdom’s primary firewall for three years, arguing that “if it isn’t broken, it’s secure.”

    The Great Debate

    Elysia is widely respected for her patience during the midnight debates in the Citadel of Ice. While Aldric argues for the latest features of the newest kernels, Elysia maintains a fortress-like focus on uptime and reliability.

    Diplomatic Stance

    In the conflict with Kingdom of Westwall, Elysia takes a defensive posture. She believes that a properly configured vault is more valuable than ten thousand swords.

    Equipment

    Elysia carries a heavy ring of iron keys, each corresponding to a physical server rack buried deep within the permafrost. It is said she can identify a hardware failure simply by the change in the castle’s ambient temperature.
